### Throughput screening — inference-only hypothetical, not a product benchmark

The previously captured protected export contained 1391 frames at 30 fps. Applying the **Stage 3 synthetic median** to every frame as a simple, serial, independent-MagicTouch scenario gives:

- Inference-only rate: `1000 / 508.078 ≈ 1.97 frames/second`, versus ~30 frames/second source clock; each synthetic inference median consumes ~15.24 video frame intervals.
- `1391 × 508.078 / 1000 = 706.736 seconds ≈ 11 minutes 47 seconds` for **segment only**.
- Adding the separately reported set-image median every frame: `1391 × (508.078 + 3.537) / 1000 ≈ 711.66 seconds ≈ 11 minutes 52 seconds`.
- For illustrative **sparse** application at 1 / 10 / 100 frames: `~0.51s / ~5.08s / ~50.81s` segment-only serial synthetic cost respectively. This is a cost sketch, not evidence sparse selection or identity matching is safe.
- These estimates exclude all existing YOLO, crop, masking, decode, render, encode, memory/thermal, and synchronization costs. They are **not** predicted full-export durations or acceptance limits.

The result corroborates the concern about a heavy independent segmenter on this target device. There is no justified product performance budget for an extra >500 ms inference per protected video frame. **Do not implement CPU full-video per-frame MagicTouch.**
