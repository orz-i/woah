# M4.5 — pose-conditioned body-part evidence

Base: `48be5914`. Research-only; no new tracker, model dependency or release policy activated.

## Method

Use Google MoveNet SinglePose Lightning float16 v4 with local LiteRT (RGB uint8 192x192, 17 y/x/confidence keypoints), [published model](https://www.kaggle.com/models/google/movenet/tfLite/singlepose-lightning-tflite-float16/1), Apache-2.0. Model archive and extracted SHA-256 are pinned in the summary/evaluator. The official tutorial documents the model: https://www.tensorflow.org/hub/tutorials/movenet. Downloaded from the public Kaggle model API; weights stay local. Pose inference uses the raw person's square crop with non-exclusive mask pixels blanked; the context ablation retains the surrounding crop for pose only. No images, embeddings or keypoints stored or sent by the evaluator.

FrameKernel takes source-coordinate keypoints, full raw observation/mask pool and current RGB. Two normalized 64x64 parts: shoulder midpoint to hip midpoint, and hip midpoint to knee midpoint. It requires current exclusive mask support at joints, confidence >=0.30, plausible axes and scale; a part needs at least 1024 supported patch samples and 20 distinct prototype cells. Samples above the upper 20% of the body box, other identities' mask pixels, image padding and weak target mask cells are excluded. The adapter also blanks currently detected face envelopes. This reduces head evidence; it does not prove removal of all undetected face pixels. No face features or pose coordinates themselves are used as identity evidence. Part-level color descriptors use square-root RGB/chroma histograms; 0287 FP32 on an otherwise neutral body-shaped canvas is an out-of-domain embedding ablation, not a trained part-specific encoder.

Use the same pinned M4.4 Basque and Swing seeds/landmarks and C9.3 Salsa for additional comparison. Initial reference bank is at most five consecutive canonical raw observations proven by the existing FrameKernel two-anchor logic. Current anonymous triplets require reciprocal raw-mask links, and are otherwise not pooled. Freeze min similarity 0.65 and two-way margin 0.05. Compare single and paired parts and equal-weight global fusion. A separate corroboration ablation requires the same selected raw index as the full clothing row's unique top candidate with row margin >=0.05; all global rivals remain in that rank. This is only retrieval evaluation, not an identity commit. A missing part stays unavailable. Sparse checks exclude the seed frame, and no later label enters matching.

M4.4 exclusive full-body models only produced a strict correct match at the later check (0288 pooled, 0287 initial-only). The earlier 175 check is new evidence. This is not yet a recovered video trajectory or demonstrated new rendered coverage.

Part availability at later source check times: Basque 5/9 have torso and both parts, Swing 15/22 have torso and both parts, Salsa 4/6 have torso and 3/6 both. Swing initial ID6 has no reliable lower-body part and cannot establish a two-part reference. Retaining image context in pose input reduces positive part availability and gives no Basque corroborated torso match. Reject that approach here. Full-body features remain necessary; the sparse part cue must not replace them.

The final parallel host comparison measures pose crop+inference median approximately 4.6–5.4 ms and part crop+0287 inference 6.3–11.1 ms per call on Basque/Swing; isolated sequential trials were approximately 3.5–3.7 ms and 4.9–5.2 ms respectively. These are local stage timings, not mobile estimates. Exact counts, timing and prior/context comparisons are in `m45-summary.json`.

## Reproduction

Build FrameKernel release. Use the existing M4.4 isolated environment and weights; extract `4.tflite` from https://www.kaggle.com/api/v1/models/google/movenet/tfLite/singlepose-lightning-tflite-float16/1/download to the local model directory as `movenet-lightning-f16-4.tflite`. The evaluator verifies its SHA-256, detector and source fixture hashes and exact M4.3 raw boxes.

```sh
python tools/privacy/evaluate_pose_parts.py --library "$LIB" \
  --fixture "$BASQUE_MANIFEST" --labels "$BASQUE_LABELS" \
  --reference-replay "$M43_BASQUE_REPLAY" --selected-id 0 \
  --model-dir "$MODEL_DIR" --output basque-parts.json
```

Repeat for Swing with `--selected-id 6 --partner-id 7`, Salsa with `--selected-id 0 --partner-id 1` and C9.3 reference labels. Use `--pose-context` for the context ablation. Raw media and weights remain local.
