## Failure analysis and algorithms

Review all fourteen M4.6 missing head samples. Several current masks have a tiny tapered crown, then a normal wider head: the old top-down trace can classify the expansion as the shoulder junction too early. The late mirror samples include an arm/sleeve joining the upper silhouette. A weak pose or ambiguous branch is not evidence of invisibility.

- Ear-level cap: use both current ear and shoulder joints with exclusive mask support and plausible anatomy. Trace a stable ear-level run up to its actual crown and down to sustained widening; reject wide, shifted or branching runs. Keep existing strong/exclusive cap support and direct-face/rival competition constraints. Pose only locates head geometry, never identity.
- Current horizontal flip retry: run the same isolated pose crop in the other orientation, map left/right keypoints into original source coordinates, and require per-joint and midpoint agreement with the original pose. A weak primary can be supplemented but a strongly inconsistent one cannot. Reuse the ear cap with the original current masks.
- Two-scale local detail: only after scene attempts fail, plan two current body crops (70% and 85% body depth). Run current local segmentation; use shared FrameKernel reprojection and owner arbitration against *all* current raw scene masks, requiring IoU >=0.65, row margin >=0.20 and rival IoU <=0.15. Require local ear caps in both views, not crop-edge lobes, with source-space cap IoU >=0.65 and current scene mask core support. Union the two current caps and preserve any supported direct selected-face measurement. Reject overlap >10% of a current detected rival face rectangle. One shared final extent/competition routine serves all three paths. Nothing learned or interpolated from past head size, masks or pose.

All three use existing LiteRT person/face/MoveNet and 0287 identity evidence; detail requires up to two additional person inferences and local face/pose inference only after scene fallback fails. No mobile timing claim.
