# M3.1 — local resegmentation of a merged current edge head

Base `561c56a9` (M3). Opt-in host research only (`--merged-edge`); the native runtime and normal algorithms remain unchanged. This is a narrow visible-top-edge recovery, not full-body instance separation or general partial-face recovery.

## Real evidence and algorithm

In the independent Salsa fixture, both people remain visible while the full-frame person detector emits one merged mask. Broad full-body and upper-body crops repeatedly yielded one merged person too. Side-biased crops recovered the target head silhouette but their lower body remained clipped. The first pair of crop plans was rejected because the second cap touched its internal crop boundary (possible artificially clipped head). The retained plan preserves the neighbor-side margin and varies far-side range and depth. Both current crops now trace the same cap extent, with current merged-mask support; a third current crop and a fresh direct detector provide a distinct rival face witness. Plan uses only the last qualified separate-pair cap and the last exact observed rival mask's direct face, each within 150 ms. Previous raw masks must match both canonical observations exactly; missing/ambiguous anchors abstain. Source annotations never enter algorithm decisions.

FrameKernel owns crop planning, cap tracing, reprojection contract, current-support/rival checks and final placement. Each local crop must contain exactly one person instance at confidence >=0.50. The packed scene-grid planes cannot claim support outside the planned crops. Both target caps must touch the actual source top, be complete connected caps with >=6 rows and no internal crop boundary contact, agree at IoU >=0.75, and have >=90% current strong support backed by the merged mask. A fresh rival detector must remain compatible with its frozen anchor and both current merged/rival masks; its face core cannot lie in the traced cap. The resulting square encloses both current caps with sampling margin and cannot cover the fresh rival face center. No changes to tracking, detector/identity clocks, projection lease, or temporal render state; recovered output cannot seed another recovery. The host square and native ellipse/mask compositor remain separate.

## Perturbations and limits

Unmodified mirrored Salsa has no single-instance merge; both target reports equal M3 (79/80 and 80/80 regions). Mirrored IDs map by initial raw boxes; labels are mirrored geometrically. Injecting synthetic raw union masks/boxes at five separate sampled frames in the original and five in the mirror (same current source image in both arms) adds no successful rescue beyond the natural original merge. One mirrored injected frame qualified for three local segmentations and a fresh rival face but the final Rust consensus refused it. Other injections do not qualify for the full gate; both arms have the same remaining coverage deficits (79/80 regions, sampled full rectangles 3/8 original and 5/8 mirror). These are detector-input perturbations, not independently filmed validation. They do not prove generalization. The extra calls are restricted to a specific complete initial pair and a current observed merged owner with a retained unobserved rival. Existing group ID2/3 still have 12/14 unresolved target frames respectively. The next work should address partial visibility in crowded scenes and recover current head evidence after longer occlusion; do not tune this edge-only experiment to those individual frames or activate mobile integration yet.

```sh

  --fixture "$SALSA_MANIFEST" --target-id 1 --credible-roots --multiperson-recovery \
  --pair-continuity --same-base-policy --pair-head --merged-edge --output salsa-front.json
python tools/privacy/audit_face_sticker_samples.py --replay salsa-front.json \
  --fixture "$SALSA_MANIFEST" --labels reports/privacy-issue47-host-2026-11-28/m3-salsa-head-labels.json \
  --case salsa_front --output front-audit.json
```
