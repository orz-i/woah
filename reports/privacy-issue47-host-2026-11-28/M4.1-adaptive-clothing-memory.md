# M4.1 — evidence-scoped adaptive clothing memory

Base `a7b31e5c` (M4). Host-only opt-in `--adaptive-clothing` on top of M4. Baseline in paired experiments is the actual M4 clothing session, not M3.3. Long-gap recovery and independent generalization remain unresolved.

## Mechanism and decisions

M4's immutable initial clothing descriptors fail when similarly dressed dancers change pose and visibility. In Basque, the selected blue-shawl/red-skirt dancer and another comparably scaled red-skirt dancer have initial descriptor similarity about 0.723. At several later source landmarks, the selected dancer's descriptor is closer to the other initial reference than to its own. Swing's two dancer seeds have similarity about 0.244, so the existing distinctive-clothing profile is useful there.

The retained candidate uses the immutable initial descriptor, a recent trusted descriptor, and a bounded bank of at most four clothing descriptors. Adaptation is limited to protected roots whose comparable-sized protected seed rival has clothing similarity >=0.70. Distinctive roots retain M4 scores and gates. All roots still compete.

Only an exact canonical current raw observation can learn: both previous and current observations must be real, the previous observation must be within 100 ms, box IoU >=0.65, raw mask IoU >=0.70, and every other current proposal and fresh prior identity must lose the raw-mask match by >=0.20. Require valid exclusive clothing evidence and initial or recent corroboration. A diverse bank entry additionally requires initial and recent evidence. No learning from predictions, duplicate raw proposals, long gaps, or new IDs. A recent feature may assist a matching current raw silhouette within 150 ms; long-gap appearance scoring uses two diverse bank witnesses and an immutable-seed floor. Existing height, travel, current ownership, and reciprocal association gates remain. No image or face descriptors retained. The explicit RGB session action 4/5 selects adaptive seed/observe; immutable action 0/1 remains unchanged, and mismatched action modes are rejected.

Three experiments:
- V1 adaptive bank for all roots: Basque ID0/1 regions 78/275 and 101/275, Swing ID7 drops to 246/300 and one correct landmark is lost. Rejected.
- V2 disables hard appearance gate for initial similar roots and uses recent fallback: Basque regions 244/275 and 237/275, but selected ID0 original source audit still 3/10 correct and six ambiguous; Swing ID7 drops to 138/300. Rejected. More regions alone are not identity improvement.
- V3 scoped adaptive model: preserves Swing, improves first continuous Basque segment with directly audited correct new observations. Retained for further research only.

## Audit semantics correction

C7.3 Basque source annotations identify a fixed selected subject but use distractor points for non-target exclusion; the distractor can be a different person across frames. Previous M4 summary counted those points as a persistent second identity, which is not supported by the source annotation schema and review. Do not interpret its 20-checkpoint combined metric as pair identity truth. The audit helper now has explicit `--selected-only` mode. It still verifies the selected raw mask at its source landmark and against the current negative point, but makes no claim about a persistent distractor identity. The original labels were not modified. The supplemental continuity label file is separate; later labels never enter inference or association.
