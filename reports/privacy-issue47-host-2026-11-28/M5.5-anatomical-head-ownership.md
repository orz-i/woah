# M5.5 — Anatomy-first current head ownership

Status: meaningful source-reviewed improvement; experimental, not accepted for release. Continues PR #48, base `9d0821d7`. All validation is local on Mac.

## Mechanism

M5.4 exposed a head/body ownership disconnect: a correct tracked body can include a neighbor's face in its raw segmentation mask, and repeating face detection in a similar crop can corroborate the wrong head. Face-seeded head geometry and temporal enlargement then preserve that mistake.

The new shared Rust path derives a current head certificate independently of a face observation. Two mapped MoveNet poses establish agreement of ear and shoulder locations, a plausible upright head-to-neck axis, and an anatomical lower scan boundary. The connected current raw silhouette supplies the crown and lateral extent; competing masks and weak support can invalidate the certificate. The two independently traced envelopes must agree. Geometry is not seeded by the previous sticker or a detected face.

Only when the old sticker center disagrees with the independent head certificate is replacement considered. A current face inside the certified envelope can contribute its extent; a spatially contradictory face is not unioned into it. The existing FrameKernel current-head competition/minimum-square solver supplies the final placement. Unresolved measured competition or an unknown nearby proposal rejects the replacement. The FFI recomputes the head certificate from current masks and poses instead of accepting a caller-provided rectangle as an ownership proof.

The mechanism is exposed via `--owned-head-experiment` in the M5.4 unified host entrypoint. It reuses scene masks/model instances, runs after existing fragment geometry and before offline recovery, and is separately comparable with the profile experiment. The host preserves canonical tracker state, legacy ellipse and trusted direct-face history. This is an anatomy-first final-placement experiment, not yet a correction to the temporal face observation clock.

## Local video results

Thirteen complete geometry replays on the M5.4 default candidate reference, with source/model hashes and current re-decoded raw boxes checked. All 13 preserve canonical identity, kind and legacy render rows. Only 53 sticker rows change: 52 Ensemble and one Basque mirror; the other 11 cases reproduce the complete reference rows. Ensemble was also freshly analyzed end-to-end through the unified entrypoint with the experiment enabled; its output candidate rows and canonical baseline reproduce the independent geometry replay exactly. The default entrypoint remains unchanged.

| Source audit | Complete head envelope before → after | Max checked neighbor overlap before → after |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ensemble fixed grid | 4/12 → 8/12 | 100% → 100% |
| Ensemble consecutive six | 0/6 → 6/6 | 100% → 0% |
| Ensemble every fourth first-pass changed sample | 0/13 → 13/13 | 100% → 100% |
| Basque mirror changed sample | 0/1 → 1/1 | 100% → 0% |
| Danzón fixed grid | 10/12 → 10/12 | 100% → 100% |
| Baganda fixed grid | 3/11 → 3/11 | not annotated |

The additional changed-sample audit is based on source-only crops, with approximate rectangles and selected nearby head envelopes. It is selection-conditioned, not a blind independent holdout or dense semantic ground truth. Body probes in the 13 changed Ensemble samples fall from 9 covered to 0; three reviewed samples show increased neighbor overlap (approximately 12%, 81%, and 100%). Their raw scene proposals lack a distinct nearby rival observation. A target mask can contain more than one person's head, so exclusivity among existing proposals cannot establish unique head ownership when a rival is missed or merged. These regressions prevent default adoption. Danzón is unchanged because its bad placements need different treatment: center agreement alone does not certify sufficient extent or neighbor exclusion.

The six-frame improvement, in contrast, is a consistent real correction: the selected head remains visible in profile while the previous placement attaches to another person below it. Independent anatomical evidence relocates the sticker to the actual head, with all six target envelopes covered and no overlap with the checked neighbor rectangles.

## Compute and next work

Ensemble standalone geometry replay: 291 scheduled frames, 153 certified caps, 521 face and 582 pose invocations, 291 repeated scene segmentations, 52 edits. Unified execution shares scene evidence; the ownership stage performs zero additional scene segmentations, and exact tensor memoization shares inference with the dense stage. Fresh combined run: 300 person, 2913 face, 3065 pose, 1337 ReID invocations; approximately 60 s on this Mac under concurrent load. The M5.4 cold Ensemble default recorded 300 person, 2905 face, 2774 pose, 1337 ReID calls. Timings are host observations, not a controlled device speed comparison.

Next: treat missing or merged nearby head evidence as an ownership problem before final placement, and move a validated direct-face rejection upstream of the temporal face clock. Reuse the same head certificate/competition primitives; avoid growing sample-specific fallback paths. Long-duration identity loss in Baganda and Basque remains open. No mobile activation or release acceptance.

## Reproduction

```sh
cargo build --release --manifest-path crates/frame-kernel/Cargo.toml
"$PY" tools/privacy/evaluate_owned_heads.py --library "$LIB" \
  --fixture "$FIXTURE" --reference-replay "$M54_UNIFIED" \
  --model-dir "$MODEL_DIR" --output "$OUTPUT"
"$PY" tools/privacy/evaluate_unified_candidate.py --library "$LIB" \
  --fixture "$FIXTURE" --target-id "$TARGET" --model-dir "$MODEL_DIR" \
  --output-dir "$OUTPUT_DIR" --owned-head-experiment
```

Use the committed M5.4 fixed-grid and continuous labels and M5.5 changed-sample labels with `audit_face_sticker_samples.py`. Exact replay hashes, per-case decisions, source-audit outcomes and verification results are in `m55-summary.json`. Media, weights and full replays remain local.
