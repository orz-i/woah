## Profile-axis head geometry

Current horizontal shoulder/ear geometry is unreliable in a side-on/leaning pose: the two ears can coincide and the head lobe merges with the shoulder in image rows. Reuse current MoveNet and exact current source raw proposal, sample the upper mask along the ear-to-shoulder axis, require complete crown and sustained lower widening, high exclusive current mask support, then compare two independently mapped current poses and cap bounds. The union is passed through the existing shared current direct/rival face placement finalizer.

A separately scoped repair applies only to a previously projected placement, fresh observed body, absent fresh selected direct face, and actual current detected rival-face conflict. The new current placement must pass the same rival-face veto and be at least 20% smaller than the old maximum radius. It replaces only host square placement, never canonical identity, historical clocks, raw face or native ellipse. Direct geometry requests are diagnostics only. The new adapter uses pinned fixture/model hashes, unique current raw box and detector replay equality; no source labels are used in automatic repair selection.

Full Danzon and Ensemble profile passes auto-select one and nine repairs respectively. Targeted source-only rectangles on all ten changed samples are reviewed *after* selection and are not a blind/dense audit.
