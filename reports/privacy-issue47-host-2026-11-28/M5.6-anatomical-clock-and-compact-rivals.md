# M5.6 — Anatomical observation clock and compact negative rivals

Host-only research, base `f3db918d`, existing PR #48. Mac local validation; GitHub CI not required. Both new experiments remain opt-in.

## Mechanism

M5.5 corrects final sticker placement but leaves the trusted direct-face clock and temporal size state contaminated by contradictory observations. The M5.6 anatomical observation FFI independently recomputes the current dual-pose/mask head certificate and returns compatible, inconclusive, or contradictory. The direct face center must be inside the padded cap and must not lie below the ear-to-shoulder axis in both current poses. The latter prevents the neck corridor from being mistaken for a face. This is evidence classification, not a claim that all rejected detections are manually verified false.

An explicit contradiction clears the host's trusted face, pixel/size and projection seeds before the region state update; the body tracker is unchanged. A subsequent contradicted projected measurement is also discarded before the update. During the resulting hold, a certified current cap can supply final coverage when the scene competition decision allows it, without renewing the direct-face clock. Inconclusive anatomical evidence preserves the legacy behavior. The optional post-placement M5.5 path is retained for comparison.

The compact-rival experiment reuses the two existing upper-body context views and current raw-mask reprojection. A compact missing proposal is admitted only when both independent views support the selected person's ear core via a separate raw mask, the compact proposal excludes that core, its scene-grid support is not already owned by a scene proposal, and its mask has a unique reciprocal match across views. Its geometry is negative fragment evidence, not a complete semantic head or new identity. No source labels enter either path. The existing fixed-extent square minimax solver compares the best position with the centered position; an unavoidable residual conflict remains explicitly unresolved. The target required extent is not clipped to remove the conflict.

## Actual results

Initial clock-only ablation rejected 24 direct and eight projected observations in Ensemble, but the legacy fallback gave only 4/6 complete coverage and 67.74% maximum labeled neighbor overlap in the previous six-frame window. This is rejected as the candidate. The improved clock + current certificate placement rejects 25 direct and eight projected observations, and retains 6/6 complete head coverage with zero checked neighbor overlap; it yields 107 anatomy-based sticker placements in Ensemble. The 13 M5.5 changed-sample labels remain 13/13 complete. Ensemble 294/300 total placements remain unchanged. A newly reviewed fixed-grid frame (225) remains incomplete and has increased checked neighbor overlap; it is not a solved case.

Thirteen current/canonical-stage complete video replays were performed in each of the clock ablations and combined compact-rival arm. Every body track/snapshot matches the M5.4 reference. The final combined current-stage arm changes geometry in four cases: Ensemble, Basque mirror, Swing ID7, original-group mirror ID5. The other nine reproduce canonical placements exactly. The four changed cases also ran freshly end-to-end through the unified entrypoint (fragment and dense/tracklet stages included). End-to-end regions: Ensemble 294, Basque mirror 77 (previously 78), Swing ID7 265, original-group mirror 105. The missing Basque mirror sample at 54 is a visible target whose body is unobserved and previously projected sticker covered the wrong head (0% target coverage in source audit). The new path does not relabel it occluded; the missing coverage remains a failure.

## Compact-rival cost and unresolved competition

Ensemble frame 200: independently observed compact rival fragment in two views, match IoU approximately 0.94 in the initial probe. The scene segmentation did not own most of that fragment. Model-derived necessary overlap floor approximately 33.88%; centered square approximately 58.18%; best square approximately 36.35%. The separate source label shows about 47.06% to 20.59% neighbor overlap with full target envelope coverage retained. This is one positive but unresolved research result, not a clearance decision. Other merged-head examples (203/222) remain undifferentiated in both local segmentation views. The compact experiment adds 214 person segmentation calls on Ensemble and changes one placement; it is not worth unconditional scheduling.

Fresh combined end-to-end Ensemble: 514 person, 2931 face, 3065 pose, 1337 ReID calls, roughly 83 s under concurrent host load. The clock arm without compact local retry uses 300 scene person calls in its current stage; exact inference memoization avoids repeat pose/face requests. No device timing is claimed. All measured local outputs, model counts and source-audit hashes are recorded in `m56-summary.json`.

## Next decision

Keep clock and compact rival experiments independently selectable. Do not promote either by threshold expansion. The clock mechanism shows genuine cross-clip benefit but missing/partial target geometry and merged rival evidence remain. Prioritize independent **head instance** evidence (including back-facing and merged bodies) and a consistent head-identity stream rather than additional post-placement corrections. Baganda and Basque long-gap identity loss are still open. No Android/iOS activation, issue closure or merge.

## Reproduction

Using pinned local model weights and fixture manifests:

```sh
cargo build --release --manifest-path crates/frame-kernel/Cargo.toml
"$PY" tools/privacy/evaluate_unified_candidate.py --library "$LIB" \
  --fixture "$FIXTURE" --target-id "$TARGET" --model-dir "$MODEL_DIR" \
  --output-dir "$OUTPUT" --anatomical-clock-experiment --owned-head-experiment
```

Add `--compact-rival-experiment` for the compact negative comparison. Compare the `canonical.json` traces for the clock stage and the `unified.json` traces for the combined outputs. Source audits use the M5.4/M5.5 labels plus `m56-clock-changed-labels.json`; media and large trace artifacts remain local. No change to default production/native code.
