## Shared identity mechanism

A continuously observed, reciprocal head path may reconnect to the existing dense body identity evidence. FrameKernel accepts either three non-overlapping temporal windows with agreeing pooled and unpooled appearance votes on the same raw body, or two separated nodes of an already certified raw-mask body tracklet. These are reused cues, not new embeddings. Competing root assignments, conflicting votes and known body merges cut the path. Reuse the existing two-second horizon for bounded offline propagation; do not cross a broken head link. A current head detection by itself cannot establish target identity. The reconnect lane fills empty placements only and never supersedes an existing M5.7 assignment or changes the canonical tracker.

The integrated head stage uses the already computed dense body evidence and current head stream. No new model, inference stage or unified command-line flag. Reuse of older cached dense traces checks producer library, reference and model hashes, source/identity rows, and equality of the old/new certified body tracklet solver; reused head streams retain the M5.7 checked fusion and reciprocal links.

## Start M6: candidate convergence decision

The identity mechanism is worth retaining. Stop new head-model and local geometry research branches. The M5.7 broad head arbitration and a missing-only policy were compared on the same M5.8 output rows (the latter preserves every original existing placement). Both retain all new recovery gains. Missing-only preserves Ensemble's older 9/9 profile audit and Swing ID7's 4/4 clock audit; broad arbitration reduces these to 7/9 and 3/4 respectively. Broad arbitration improves Danzon 10/12 to 12/12 and original-group mirror 2/5 to 4/5 on the other audited grids. Neither policy has release-grade evidence. These are measured-row policy ablations, not newly executed full pipelines.

For the next single-configuration M6 replay, choose the canonical/fragment/offline chain, M5.6 anatomical observation clock and M5.8 nano independent head arbitration/reconnect. Exclude the compact-rival retry (214 extra Ensemble segmentations for one partially resolved case), the separate M5.5 post-placement pass (zero modifications on all thirteen M5.6 clock-cap replays), the unapproved profile experiment, and the medium/head-only model controls.
