# M4.2 — Independent body pixel motion and merged-instance quarantine

Base `1f28408f` (M4.1). Explicit host-only `--motion-clothing` (action 6/7). Existing immutable/adaptive actions and native consumers remain unchanged. This is a verified merge-quarantine experiment, not a stable long-gap re-identification algorithm.

## Source investigation

Basque's selected blue-shawl dancer remains correctly associated before crossing another dancer. At the first real merge, the current detection contains approximately 85% and 95% of the two preceding distinct instance foreground masks. The baseline assigns the single combined body to ID0, refreshing its canonical bounds and face observation. It then receives a fragment and loses the full identity after the actual occlusion.

Two diagnostic point-tracklet experiments used masked body texture patches and forward/backward correspondence, at 2x proto grid and half-source resolution respectively. For example, the half-source target tracklet starting with 41 points at relative frame 70 decays to 15, 8, 4, 2 in the next four samples. A fresh 22-point seed at 73 decays to 6 and 2. These traces cannot establish a trustworthy path through full occlusion. Source imagery confirms the dancer is heavily hidden at the interruption. Do not interpret a few surviving points or a nearby observed identity as proof of the target's reappearance. The local diagnostic scripts and hashes are recorded in the summary.

## Implemented shared algorithm

FrameKernel now derives a bounded reduced RGB frame (longest side <=480) and an exclusive raw-mask owner map. It keeps one immediately previous frame in memory for this explicitly enabled mode; misses/skips invalidate it. Body-only seed patches come from the interior 18–83% height band, entirely supported by the previous mask at >=160 and exclusively owned across all raw instances. At most 64 separated textured points per prior owner are evaluated with a bounded 7x7 RGB patch search, a spatially distinct second-best margin, and a forward/backward agreement within one grid pixel. No face descriptor, image file, or cross-video identity store is produced.

The current merge preflight requires two exact real observed masks from the preceding frame within 100 ms, each at least 70% contained in one current raw proposal, pairwise previous raw IoU <=0.20, and at least one protected initial similar-clothing root. Only then run pixel correspondence. Each contributing owner needs >=4 current exclusive votes to the same candidate and at least 60% of its validated matches. Flat or repeating texture, stale frames, and ambiguous raw ownership cannot authorize quarantine. No positive identity commit is created from motion evidence.

Confirmed merged raw detections enter an explicit frame-local quarantine set; every canonical association and insertion path excludes them. No fabricated matched owner, appearance update, or new ID is generated from that merged proposal. Other current proposals continue through M4.1 unchanged. The host trace exposes raw index, prior identity and current correspondence count for audit.
