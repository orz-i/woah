The actual source image shows the raised hand at the old region, and the selected head is obscured or uncertain. The rejected primary would otherwise create a fresh observation clock and propagate the incorrect region for two further samples. The same source also has a true crowded face with strong owner support (ID3) where both local probes agree. The broad diagnostic of other overlaps found no changes.

FrameKernel compares 16 current face-core samples in each raw mask; require >=4 distinct cells, >=12 foreground samples in both selected and rival, and a rival mean byte score at least 20 above selected. This is an evidence heuristic, not calibrated confidence. Only such observations in a crowded scene trigger two bounded current-image crops. Keep the original measurement if at least one local current detector agrees in center, size and current owner mask support. Otherwise the disputed observation cannot refresh the trusted face clock. For the next <=150ms, a weak body estimate whose core is likewise rival-dominated is marked unresolved. This is not proof of invisibility and the older good root is preserved. All raw tracker observations remain untouched. Host requires the exact current canonical observed mask and >=3 initial protected roots. Scoped candidate only; no new model or blanket mask veto.

A preflight ablation originally retried every strong overlap, including ID3 and three Swing samples, but all four had stronger selected mask evidence. Move the already-required dominance test ahead of inference; final output geometry is identical to the broad trial and those eight unnecessary face calls are avoided. One qualifying event remains, two additional current face calls. No device timing measured.
