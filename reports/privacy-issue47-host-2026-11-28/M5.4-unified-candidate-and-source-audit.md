# M5.4 — Unified host candidate and source head ownership audit

Status: host candidate integrated and replayed; **release acceptance not proven**. PR #48 remains draft. The release library is the unchanged M5.3 FrameKernel library. This checkpoint integrates the existing algorithms and reduces repeated inference; it does not introduce a new Rust tracking or head estimator. Mac local verification is the research-stage acceptance evidence. GitHub CI is not a prerequisite for this checkpoint. No native integration, deployment change, or merge.

## Unified pipeline

`tools/privacy/evaluate_unified_candidate.py` shares decoded scene evidence, model instances and exact-input inference results across existing FrameKernel-backed paths. The M5.1 canonical tracker owns identities. The M5.2 two-anchor fragment repair has priority over offline re-entry: neither may change canonical tracks or their raw ownership evidence. Dense analysis runs only when an unobserved tracked target has an empty placement; it is still a potentially expensive research path. Final placement has one source lane, raw owner evidence where available, square geometry and required extent where available.

The M5.1 projected-head / M5.3 competition path is **off by default**, preserved as an explicit comparison (`--profile-experiment`). Existing standalone tools remain usable. The interpreter memo is bounded and keyed by exact input tensors; only raw inference outputs are reused. Across targets, the existing dense-evidence validation binds reuse to the fixture, library, models and all-root canonical evidence.

## Source-reviewed quality

At the existing one-second body landmark grid, 35 approximate source head envelopes were reviewed across Baganda, Ensemble and Danzón. Some previously reviewed labels were reused. Labels are not passed to tracking/inference and are bound to fixture and frame hashes. An additional six consecutive Ensemble frames were reviewed around a failure. These are existing independent holdout recordings, not new recordings. Body probes are selected body points, not necessarily torso segmentation. Neighbor annotations are selected visible head envelopes, not exhaustive semantic annotations.

| Source | Correct body checks | Complete head envelope (M3.3) | M4.2 | M5.4 default | Minimum coverage (M5.4) | Maximum neighbor overlap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Baganda | 3/11 | 4/11 | 1/11 | 3/11 | 0% | not annotated on grid |
| Ensemble | 12/12 | 3/12 | 4/12 | 4/12 | 0% | 100% |
| Danzón | 12/12 | 9/12 | 10/12 | 10/12 | 58% | 100% |

Swing body checkpoints remain 23/24. The six consecutive Ensemble head labels have 0/6 complete coverage and max neighbor overlap 100% for every compared stage. This reveals a body/head ownership disconnect: the selected body can remain correctly identified while the head of a competing person is covered and the target head is missed. A region count, even 300/300, cannot establish protection. The M5.3 profile edits reduce overlap in previously selected examples but do not improve the broader fixed-grid and continuous audit results. Retain these edits as an experiment rather than a default. Full-video semantic identity-switch and visible-head-ground-truth evaluation remains outstanding.

## Next algorithm decision

Do not extend the existing per-frame competition repairs to more isolated failures. The next research focus is a consistent, target-owned head observation derived from the current person's geometry and temporal evidence, competing with all visible head candidates before final placement. In parallel, re-evaluate long-gap association on Baganda and Basque; the existing dense ReID path does not justify unconditional use. Require independent source-video evidence of identity continuity, visible-head coverage, neighbor exclusion and realistic compute cost before mobile integration.
