A further discrete junction qualification is attempted only when the previous direct cap paths fail. Where two current rows substantiate the branch, retain the actual intersection of the first widened row with the preceding head run. Require a complete head cap containing the direct detector center, with the original aspect qualification unchanged. Preserve the measured/stabilized ellipse and sampling margin. No change to detector preference, square sticker geometry, identity, ROI schedule, leases, or #47.

Earlier candidates are not adopted: one-row removal reduced some peaks but missed others; weaker cap confirmation reduced the ratio but allowed a partial cap; a two-dimensional enclosing-circle search that preserved all points reduced some radii but worsened the full-clip ratio. Final host candidate with exact Android saved raw/temporal geometry and matched CPU masks reduces right major-radius ratio 1.405577 -> 1.279011. The original, AVC and detector-drop host traces are unchanged; other host metrics and rejected variants are in the summary. These are geometry-only comparisons, not actual video results.
