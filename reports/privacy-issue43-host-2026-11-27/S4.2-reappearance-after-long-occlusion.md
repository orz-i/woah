# S4.2 — reappearance after prolonged group occlusion

Status: **visible reappearance recovered on the host; remaining partially visible and unlabeled occlusion frames not proven; no mobile integration.** Baseline 88d9c9a9.

## Root cause and algorithm

The second selected dancer (ID 3, striped top) is partly occluded by the foreground dancer. Frame 96 commits a lower-body-only box, but no current face. Later the target appears on the right of the foreground person while the predicted box remains left. The last direct face root is frame 95. A local geometry-only recovery within 950ms cannot bridge the spatial shift and time gap.

Add a separate experimental coverage-only branch in FrameKernel:
- Quantize RGB and chromaticity/luminance distributions of the garment band below a currently detected face. Sample exclusively supported current instance pixels; reject other current masks at the face center and require enough garment samples. Do not sample the face as an appearance feature. No raw pixels retained by Rust.
- Freeze at least two distinct, >=100ms-spaced direct observations from within 500ms of the last root and available other raw-body descriptors from those frames. Compare the best two target scores against every available historical rival and current candidate, including candidates committed to another owner. The current selected candidate must be unassigned to a foreign owner and have a current detector face and mask. Ambiguous same-clothing candidates, invalid evidence, missing detection, or insufficient samples abstain. This does not establish identity, an exemption, or renew any previous lease.
- The experimental lane has a separate absolute age >950ms and <=2s, constrained body and face scale/travel, and is only attempted for a retained occluded/reacquiring track. It generates a new current-frame square only; no stale projection, tracker mutation, or recursive use of the coverage result.
- A small profile face can leave part of the crown exposed. Trace the connected upper mask rows from the current face and extend only the required upper envelope with a one-cell margin, preserving the previous direct requirements. Disconnected fragments do not extend the crown.
