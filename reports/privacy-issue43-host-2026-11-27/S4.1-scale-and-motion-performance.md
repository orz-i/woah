## Algorithm
- A connected widening can recede by a prototype cell on the confirmation row. Keep the strict first widening threshold; confirm the second row by sustained relative width and previous-run overlap. A transient branch that returns to normal width remains ambiguous.
- For an estimated (not direct) non-edge head, a qualified full cap can reanchor an already-overlapping weak center that falls below the cap. Require the old ellipse to reach the lower central band and retain its radii. The cap itself remains required. Do not move direct or clipped-edge evidence, or accept a distant cap.
- Genuine direct growth is not capped: synthetic double-scale silhouette and temporal size test verifies immediate growth.
- Separable gray patch coordinate calculation: calculate 15 source columns and 15 rows once rather than all 225 coordinate pairs. Keep Float32 operation order, row-major Float64 normalization, search lattice and thresholds unchanged. Scalar oracle and real before/after ABI outputs match.
